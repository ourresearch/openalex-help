1.  Search for your institution by typing it into the search bar.
2.  Select your institution; this will create a filter.
3.  Under “Stats,” look for a panel labeled “open access”. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “open access” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows the percentage of your institution’s outputs that are Open Access.

[Learn more about Open Access (OA) in OpenAlex](/docs/open-access/)
