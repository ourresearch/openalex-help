Once you have a search results set, with whatever searches and filters you've chosen, you can see a count of the total number of citations received by all of the works in that set:

-   Under “Stats,” look for a panel listing the total number of citations. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “citations sum” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows the sum of citations received by your institution’s works. 
-   To get the average number of citations per work, you can divide this number by the total number of works.
