Institutional supporters — [Member, Member+, and Partner plans](/access/pricing/) — get access to three tools. This page covers activating each one. For the other ways supporters shape OpenAlex (advisory board nominations, quarterly roundtables), see [Getting involved](/how-to/getting-involved/).

## Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard shows OpenAlex usage data for your institution: how many of your users are on OpenAlex, and the API calls made by your research community.

1. Identify the users at your institution who should see this data (typically economic buyers or e-resource librarians).
2. Have each of them create an account at [openalex.org](https://openalex.org) ("Sign up", top right).
3. Send their names and email addresses to [kyle@openalex.org](mailto:kyle@openalex.org), and we'll enable dashboard access on their accounts.

## Affiliation Editor

The Affiliation Editor lets you curate the affiliation strings OpenAlex matches to your institution — removing erroneous matches and adding missed ones — with your changes live in OpenAlex within about two days. (For what it does and how it fits into fixing affiliation errors generally, see [Affiliations](/access/fixing-errors/affiliations/).)

To activate it, contact [kyle@openalex.org](mailto:kyle@openalex.org). We provide group training and video resources first, then enable the tool on specific accounts at your institution. We periodically monitor curation accuracy to keep quality high.

## Unsub

[Unsub](https://unsub.org) gives you data-driven forecasts of the true cost and value of your journal packages to guide subscription decisions — it's used by hundreds of libraries worldwide.

Send the names and email addresses of up to 3 users at your institution (typically collections librarians) to [kyle@openalex.org](mailto:kyle@openalex.org), and we'll create their logins. We provide [comprehensive online documentation](https://docs.unsub.org/) and shared training videos, but not direct Unsub support; if you want consulting help with analyses and implementation, we recommend Eric Schares ([scharesdatascience@gmail.com](mailto:scharesdatascience@gmail.com)), an experienced Unsub super-user.
