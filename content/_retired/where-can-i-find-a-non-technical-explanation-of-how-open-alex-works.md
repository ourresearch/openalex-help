A few places that might be helpful:

-   [help.openalex.org](/how-to/) is a growing knowledge base of non-technical information on OpenAlex. Entries are created based on user requests and common questions we get
-   we host monthly webinars that are typically more geared to non-technical users. check out upcoming and previous webinars [here](https://openalex.org/events) 
-   Our YouTube channel [https://www.youtube.com/@OurResearch](https://www.youtube.com/@OurResearch) hosts the webinar videos, but also quick hit tutorials and presentations from our users on how they use OpenAlex
-   we host weekly one-hour Office Hours where any user can come to ask questions about OpenAlex. sign up for one [here](https://openalex.org/events)

If you have other suggestions about other ways we can make non-technical explanations more accessible (ideas for webinars, new knowledge base entries, etc.), let us know at [support@openalex.org](mailto:support@openalex.org)
