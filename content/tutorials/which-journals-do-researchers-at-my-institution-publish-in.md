1.  Search for your institution by typing it into the search bar
2.  Select your institution; this will create a filter.
3.  Filter for only works that are in journals: click the “Add filter” button ![](/images/zendesk/24346632073367.png), then type “source type” and click on that filter. Select “journal” from the list that pops up.
4.  Under “Stats,” look for a panel labeled “source”. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “source” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows a count of your institution’s outputs for the top journals. Click “More…” to see the full list.
