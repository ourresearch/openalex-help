1.  Search for your institution by typing it into the search bar.
2.  Select your institution; this will create a filter.
3.   Under “Stats,” look for a panel labeled “institution”. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “institution” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows a count of your institution’s outputs and the count by collaborating institution. Your institution will be at the top of the list, and all collaborating institutions will be under. Click “More…” to see the full list.
