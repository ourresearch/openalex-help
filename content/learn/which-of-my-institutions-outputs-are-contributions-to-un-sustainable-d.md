1.  Search for your institution by typing it into the search bar.
2.  Select your institution; this will create a filter.
3.  Under “Stats,” look for a panel labeled “Sustainable Development Goal”. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “Sustainable Development Goal” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows a count of your institution’s outputs by collaborating institution. Click “More…” to see the full list. 
4.  You can select any of the SDGs to see the works that are contributing to that goal.
