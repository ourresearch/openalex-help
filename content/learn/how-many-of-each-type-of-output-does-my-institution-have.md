1.  Search for your institution by typing it into the search bar
2.  Select your institution; this will create a filter.
3.  Under “Stats,” look for a panel labeled “type”. If you don’t see it, click the plus “+” symbol next to Stats, and select “type” to add it to the Stats dashboard. This panel shows a count of your institution’s outputs by type. Click “More…” to see the full list.
