You may want to remove from your analysis all works that have been retracted.

1.  After you already have a filter or set of filters applied, click the “Add filter” button ![](/images/zendesk/24346939938711.png), then type “retracted” and click on that filter. 
2.  Use the dropdown box next to this newly created filter to select “is not”. A filter is now applied that removes retracted works from the results.
