Quarterly roundtable meetings are a benefit for all institutional supporters (Member, Member+, and Partner). They're open-forum discussions of priorities for future development roadmaps, held directly with our product team.

Meetings happen every three months. Calendar invites are sent to each institution's supporter contacts; additional attendees from your institution are welcome — just [let us know](mailto:kyle@openalex.org).
