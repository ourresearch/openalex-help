Nominating candidates for the Community Advisory Board is a benefit for all institutional supporters (Member, Member+, and Partner). The 12-member board helps guide OpenAlex's direction.

We replace 6 advisory board members annually. The next election will be in December 2026 for January 2027 seats, and we'll put out a call for nominations from supporters in Fall 2026.
