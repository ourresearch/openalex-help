The Admin Dashboard is a benefit for all institutional supporters (Member, Member+, and Partner). It gives you OpenAlex usage data for your institution: see how many users at your institution are using OpenAlex, and track API calls made by your research community.

To activate it:

1.  Identify the users at your institution who should have access to this information (typically economic buyers or e-resource librarians).
2.  Have each of them create an account by going to [openalex.org](https://openalex.org) and clicking "Sign up" at the top right.
3.  Contact us at [kyle@openalex.org](mailto:kyle@openalex.org) with their names and email addresses, and we'll enable their OpenAlex accounts for dashboard access.
