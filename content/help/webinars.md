**Innovations Enabled by OpenAlex (April 2026)**

In this webinar moderated by OpenAlex CEO and Co-Founder Jason Priem, we'll hear from three tech companies on the innovations that they've created built off OpenAlex data. (1) Kiin Bio: The pioneering AI-native platform that unifies drug discovery tools to design experiments, analyse data, and accelerate research with Virtual Scientists that integrate 100+ specialised tools into one intelligent workflow. (2) FirstIgnite builds AI Software to increase partnership opportunities for universities, hospitals, non-profits and foundations. (3) Jimma University & Open Development & Education are leveraging OpenAlex to develop Ethiopia and Africa's first open scientific knowledge graph.
