We don't currently index patents and so you can't search for them in OpenAlex. We continue to receive feedback from users on features to develop and so far indexing patents has remained low on the priorities for our users and premium subscribers. If that changes, we can expand the scope of OpenAlex to include patents. So it that's a priority for you, let us know more about why by emailing support@openalex.org
