OpenAlex's data ingest pipeline—including [getting works;](https://help.openalex.org/hc/en-us/articles/24347019383191) finding affiliations and citations; and tagging [aboutness](https://help.openalex.org/hc/en-us/articles/24859286130583)—is complex and changes frequently. It is not currently comprehensively documented, but we do have an internal dashboard measuring different aspects of how the pipeline is running over time. In the interest of openness, we provide a public link to this dashboard. We can't provide much support or answer questions about it, but it may prove useful if you have any questions about how things our going with our pipeline. [View the dashboard here.](http://unpaywall-metabase.herokuapp.com/public/dashboard/8e114521-b74b-4e6c-bba8-3a8fc573fb64)
