**Member+** is the middle annual plan, at **$10,000/year** — for organizations that keep their own copy of OpenAlex, or push enough API traffic that the Member budget is tight. It includes **everything in [Member](/access/member/)**, plus the benefits below. To buy or ask questions, email [sales@openalex.org](mailto:sales@openalex.org).

## Everything in Member, plus:

### $100/day API budget

Every day, your organization gets $100 of API usage — worth $36,500 over a year, a 73% saving compared to buying the same usage pay-as-you-go.

### Daily sync

Keep your own copy of OpenAlex fresh, two ways: a **complete daily snapshot** — the whole database, rebuilt and published every day — and **sync filters on the API** that return everything new or changed since any date. See [Sync](/access/sync/) for how it works, including how deletions and merged entities behave.

### Basic support

Ticket-based help from our team for critical API bugs that have a clear resolution. (Data-quality issues aren't covered at this tier — for full support on all issues, see [Partner](/access/partner/).)

## Also included (from Member)

The [admin dashboard](/how-to/supporter-tools/#how-do-i-activate-the-admin-dashboard), [community representation](/how-to/getting-involved/#supporter-roundtables), and — for academic and government institutions — the [affiliation editor](/how-to/supporter-tools/#how-do-i-activate-the-affiliation-editor) and [Unsub access](/how-to/supporter-tools/#how-do-i-activate-unsub). See the [Member page](/access/member/) for details on each.

## Add-ons and upgrades

Any plan can add the [PDF sync service](/access/pricing/#the-pdf-sync-add-on) — persistent sync of our full open-access PDF archive to your own storage. Need a larger budget still, full support, or power-user accounts? See [Partner](/access/partner/).
